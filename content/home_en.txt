ScanWaitWales is an independent, volunteer-run tool and is not affiliated with the NHS. We use publicly available diagnostic waiting-time data published by [StatsWales](https://stats.gov.wales/en-GB/3c2720e6-95ae-4eeb-9e88-ed74bb40c6b9/start#about) and compiled by Digital Health and Care Wales (DHCW). We do not have access to individual patient records nor do we store any information you provide us.

Official waiting-time statistics have significant reporting limitations and issues, which we actively work to clean and manage, as seen in our [Data Issues page](/data-quality). In particular, the public dataset does not separate referrals by clinical priority (such as Urgent or Routine), which heavily affects how long an individual patient waits.

Because of this, the figures shown here represent an estimate of overall waiting times rather than a prediction for your individual appointment. ScanWaitWales is best used to explore general trends and compare performance across hospitals and health boards.
